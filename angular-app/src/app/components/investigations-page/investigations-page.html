Investigations

Longer cases beyond the five missions: six checkpoints each, every answer recorded under the investigation's name. A correct answer opens the next checkpoint. One investigation is live at a time — when one is, the banner under the menu names it, and its checkpoints open below. The rest wait their turn. Each checkpoint's evidence query and question appear with Behind the scenes (top right); a checkpoint offers to switch it on if it is off.
